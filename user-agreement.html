User Agreement

Effective 29 October 2026

This agreement covers your use of Wonez on your own device. Wonez helps you file choral scores, mark your voice part, follow a synthesized part cue, keep a sing-back take, mark attendance, and generate a workbook PNG.

The local account

One-Tap Login creates a session on this device. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. Log Out ends the session and leaves your programmes on the device. Delete Account removes the local account and the programmes, media, workbook files, and Credits stored with it. You can also use Clear All Data in Settings. That erase is permanent on this install, including unused Credits.

Your material

You are responsible for the scores, photos, PDFs, and recordings you bring into the app. Wonez does not distribute published sheet music and does not claim rights in your files. Practice pages generated as a sample binder are blank local paper, not a published engraving. The part cue is a synthesized tone. It is not a recording of a copyrighted performance, and the app does not grade your singing.

Credits

Previewing a workbook is free. Confirming generation spends 1 Credit and writes one PNG in the app. Credit packs are consumable Apple in-app purchases. Credits stay on this device and are not restored after deletion, Clear All Data, or removal of the app. Refunds are handled by Apple. A revocation removes only the unused part of that grant.

Contact

Questions about this agreement are handled in the in-app Support page under Settings.
