Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Wonez is a choral part practice workbook for the device you are using. This policy describes the local session, what the app stores on that device, and when it asks for the camera, photo library, or microphone. Wonez does not upload your scores, recordings, attendance, or workbook files to a Wonez server.

Local sign-in

One-Tap Login opens a session stored only on this device. Wonez keeps a device identifier and a session token in local app preferences. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. That identifier is not sent to a Wonez account server.

Log Out removes the session token. Programmes, scores, recordings, and Credits stay on the device. Delete Account removes the local identifier, the session token, programmes, score pages, recordings, workbook files, and Credits from this install.

Data on this device

Programme names, concert details, score pages, voice-part choices, practice counts, sing-back recordings, written pass notes, rehearsal attendance, workbook PNG files, and the Credit ledger are stored in the app sandbox. Preferences such as the joined state and the last programme are stored in local app preferences. Removing the app, Delete Account, or Clear All Data deletes this local data. Clear All Data also removes remaining Credits. A new starter balance can be issued on the device after that wipe. Purchased Credits are not restored.

Camera

The camera is requested only after you tap Scan Score. Photos you accept are copied into the app sandbox for the score you are filing. Wonez does not use the camera in the background.

Photo library

Photo library access is requested only after you tap Choose Photo, so you can import score pictures. Wonez copies the items you select and does not change your library. Add-only access is requested only after you tap Save Image, and only to write that workbook PNG.

Microphone

The microphone is requested only after you tap Record. The recording stays with that score on this device. If you decline, you can keep a written pass note instead. Wonez does not score pitch or upload audio.

Purchases

Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Wonez keeps the resulting Credit balance on this device. The balance is not restored after you delete the app, clear its data, or delete the local account. Apple handles payment and refund requests. A refund or revocation reduces only the unused portion of that grant. Workbooks already generated stay on the device until you delete them.

What we do not collect

Wonez does not request tracking permission and does not read an advertising identifier. Wonez does not include advertising. There is no Wonez analytics server receiving your scores or recordings.

Contact

Questions about this policy are handled in the in-app Support page under Settings.
